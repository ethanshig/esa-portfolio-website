About
I’m a Bachelor of Science in Sustainable Design at the University of Illinois, where my work explores the intersection of architecture, sustainability, and ecological resilience. My involvement in the Campus Honors Program, James Scholar Program, and Dean’s List reflects my dedication to learning, while balanced by real-world experience from various work opportunities. from researching human impact on biodiversity in Paraguay to interning in Architecture at RATIO.
Through my variety of experiences I have developed a deep appreciation for the relationship between built environments and natural systems. I strive to integrate this understanding into my design process, balancing technical precision with environmental responsibility. My experience in digital fabrication, construction documentation,
and environmental analysis allows me to approach projects with both innovation and sustainability in mind.
I am always looking for opportunities to grow and collaborate. Feel free to reach out to me at ethanshig@gmail.com or +1 (217) 778-0298.

3 Little Pigs
Ever wonder why the 3 little pigs didn’t stick together? Me too.
This wall assembly is designed for compatibility and sustainability. Its traditional stick framing makes it easily adaptable to conventional construction methods, while its R-52 straw bale insulation ensures the health and thermal comfort of its occupants, along with incredible energy savings.

Mylo To-Go
In a world drowning in plastic waste, we turned to nature for a solution. Our mycelium based containers aren’t just biodegradable; they’re grown, not manufactured. Designed to replace single-use Styrofoam, our containers decompose naturally, leaving no trace. 
The creation of MYLO entailed collaboration as a small team to explore the full development cycle of mycelium based fabrication. Contributed to every step of the process: form development, mold fabrication, mycelium cultivation, visual documentation, and iteration.
