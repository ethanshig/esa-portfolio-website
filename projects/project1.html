3 Little Pig Wall Assembly

Type Sustainable Construction
Year 2025
Location University of Illinois
Role Design & Development

Project Overview

Ever wonder why the 3 little pigs didn't stick together? Me too. This wall assembly is designed for compatibility and sustainability. Its traditional stick framing makes it easily adaptable to conventional construction methods, while its R-52 straw bale insulation ensures the health and thermal comfort of its occupants, along with incredible energy savings.

Challenge

How can we integrate natural, sustainable materials like straw bale insulation into conventional construction methods while achieving exceptional thermal performance and maintaining affordability?

Solution

The design bridges traditional stick framing with innovative natural insulation, creating a wall assembly that contractors can easily work with while delivering R-52 insulation values. This hybrid approach makes sustainable building practices accessible without requiring specialized construction knowledge or techniques.

Key Features

  • R-52 straw bale insulation providing exceptional thermal performance
  • Compatible with traditional stick framing construction methods
  • Natural, renewable materials promoting occupant health
  • Significant energy savings over conventional insulation systems
  • Easy integration into standard building practices
  • Carbon-sequestering agricultural waste material (straw)

Outcomes & Impact

This wall assembly demonstrates that high-performance sustainable building doesn't require abandoning conventional construction methods. By designing for compatibility, the system makes exceptional thermal performance and occupant health accessible to standard residential construction.

The R-52 insulation value significantly exceeds typical residential wall assemblies (R-13 to R-21), offering substantial energy savings and improved thermal comfort. The use of agricultural waste material (straw) transforms a byproduct into a valuable building resource while sequestering carbon.
