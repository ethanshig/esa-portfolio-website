AKNOWLEDGMENT

In 1979, Warren Robinett hid his name in the game Adventure for the Atari 2600—the world's first video game easter egg. Why? Because Atari didn't credit its developers.

This site was built with Claude Code, an AI assistant. Like all generative AI, it builds on the work of countless people: writers, programmers, designers, etc. whose contributions are often invisible. This tool helped me build this site, but it stands on the creation of others.

The irony in this is especially rich: using a tool trained on uncredited labor to recognize uncredited labor. That's why this page exists. To acknowledge the paradox.

And to serve as a signature: Hidden, but not invisible. A reminder that every project, whether a building, a website, or a line of code, carries the fingerprints of those who made it, and those who made them possible.
