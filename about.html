Design Philosophy

Regenerative Design

I strive to create buildings that go beyond "doing less harm" to actively regenerating ecosystems and enriching the natural environment.

Community-Centered

Great design emerges from deep listening to communities. I prioritize participatory processes that center the needs and wisdom of residents.

Circular Materials

Every material choice matters. I advocate for locally sourced, low-carbon materials and designs that facilitate deconstruction and material reuse.

Passive-First Approach

Technology can't solve everything. I prioritize passive strategies—orientation, natural ventilation, daylighting—before adding mechanical systems.
