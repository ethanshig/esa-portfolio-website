# The Map reports, it does not advise

The Session already records the Interests a reader signalled through their choices, and the
Map could plainly use them to highlight Regions the reader seems drawn to. We decided it will
not. The Map shows where a reader has been and nothing about where they should go.

## Considered Options

Interest-weighting is the obvious use of data that is sitting right there, which is exactly
why this needs recording — someone will propose it again. It is rejected because the tour is
built on the claim that the story a reader assembles by choosing is *theirs*. A surface that
quietly ranks destinations by inferred preference makes that false, and it makes the Map a
recommender wearing a map's clothes.

## Consequences

This also fixes where the reader's "souvenir" lives. A Map that totals up what you saw is a
reproach while you still have somewhere to go, so it is kept out of the tour — but on
`transcript.html`, where the reader has finished, the same rendering is the right artifact.
The Map is therefore interactive in the tour and static on the transcript, and it is never
personalised in either place.
