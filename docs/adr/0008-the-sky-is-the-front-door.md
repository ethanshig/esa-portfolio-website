# The Sky is the front door

**Superseded 2026-11-24 (Ethan):** the 15 Dec submission is carried by a new site in a new repo, not this one. The Sky continues there as the front door, with these decisions carried into its plan; on this repo nothing replaces the Plates. Below is the decision as it stood.

The front door stops being a conventional grid. A reader arrives in the **Sky**: all of Ethan's work
as one Light cone (height is time, the tip is now), standing inside at the tip, with the cone itself
one toggle away. Records are Stars, authored Crossings are the lines flown between them, and
second-person Narration opens it. This supersedes ADR-0006's "conventional at first glance" for the
front door only; inside a project, the story stays a layer over the page as ADR-0006 and ADR-0007 say.

## Why

Ethan's reason is the metaphor itself: he wanted to be an astronaut, and the point is travel,
entering systems and going between them. A throwaway prototype (branch `prototype/sky`) showed that
arriving at now, picking a light and flying a Crossing reads as a portfolio rather than a toy, and he
chose it over the Plates for the 15 Dec Media Lab gate, three weeks out.

## Consequences

**The Plates index becomes the fallback**: the front door for no-WebGL, phones and reduced motion, as
the Document is for a Sheet. It is already built, so it is also the safety net: if the Sky is not
working by **8 Dec**, the Plates go live for the gate and the Sky follows after.

**Audience does not apply in the Sky.** For 15 Dec the Sky holds only the Media Lab's three pieces
(15, 17 and 23), so there is nothing to frame.

**ADR-0005's no-invention rule returns, amended**: the Sky never shows more work than exists, and an
Idea in the future cone is never passed off as finished work.
